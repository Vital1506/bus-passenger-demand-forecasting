Scheduled daily-count feed

An approved company scheduler can send route-day counts to POST /api/data/ingest with the bearer key. Matching route and service-date rows are safely replaced, and the current model refreshes the forecast. New rows do not retrain the model automatically; schedule training separately.

Example integration request
POST /api/data/ingest
Authorization: Bearer <operator-key>
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "source_system": "ticketing_daily_export",
  "records": [
    {"service_date": "2026-11-09", "route_id": "R10", "passengers": 1240}
  ]
}

MODEL QUALITY

Model & validation

Time-based evaluation

In plain language: the model looks at daily counts you provide, learns patterns such as weekday differences, and estimates future counts. A place name by itself does not reveal how many people actually ride a bus.

1. Record what happened

Enter one route and one date at a time, or import an existing daily-count file. Do not guess missing passenger counts.

2. Build enough history

The prototype needs about 118 continuous dates per route before its time-based model check can run.

3. Forecast and explain

It compares the machine-learning model with a simple weekday baseline and shows an approximate error range.

4. Review with people

Compare estimates with actual counts and service changes. Sample forecasts are for learning, not operational decisions.

What this score means

WAPE is total absolute forecast error divided by total observed boardings across backtest periods. Compare scores across the same dates and data definition.

Before operational use

Validate with your service calendar, disruptions, route changes, and a shadow run. Forecast intervals are approximate historical error ranges.
