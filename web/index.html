Waiting for service

This page checks the connected service every 60 seconds while open. New daily counts update forecasts after ingestion and model refresh.

About live data

This dashboard can refresh against its API, but it does not currently receive live ticketing or vehicle telemetry. To make forecasts operationally real time, connect an approved ticketing export, AVL/GTFS-Realtime feed, or company data platform and define its update schedule.

What this score means

WAPE is total absolute forecast error divided by total observed boardings across backtest periods. Compare scores across the same dates and data definition.

Before operational use

Validate with your service calendar, disruptions, route changes, and a shadow run. Forecast intervals are approximate historical error ranges.
